How to use

One dropper. Once a day. No fuss.

  1. 1

    Shake well. Fill the dropper to 1 mL.

  2. 2

    Mix into juice, milk or a smoothie. Orange juice and berry smoothies hide the herbal taste best.

  3. 3

    Give once daily with food for 2–3 weeks, then take a break.

What to expect

Every child is different. Many parents notice settled tummies and better appetites within 1–2 weeks of daily use.*

First time? Start with half a dropper for the first few days.

Safety & who it’s for

  • For children 3+. Not for children under 3, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

  • Contains tree nut (black walnut).

  • Stop use and talk to your doctor if your child has vomiting, diarrhea, stomach pain, blood in stool, changes in urination, or any unusual reaction.

  • Talk to your pediatrician before use if your child takes medication or has a health condition.

  • [VERIFY: thujone-free wormwood spec] Line to read once confirmed: “Made with thujone-free wormwood.”
