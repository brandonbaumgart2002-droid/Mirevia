## Patterns worth copying
- Every headline: plain first half + accented second half in the brand accent colour, italic.
- Every proof has a "name · dose" format; nothing vague.
- Buy box stack: badges → headline → bold-lead bullets → ship line → bundle → plan cards → ATC with guarantee → quick questions.
- Price shown three ways in the selected plan: bundle price, per-day price, "You save $X" pill.
- Subscribe card pre-selected with a "Recommended" tag and an expandable rewards panel.
- Timeline ends visibly at the guarantee window.
- Who-it's-for tiles are interactive (tap a tile, card below changes).
- Sticky bar uses "Choose bundle" (scrolls to buy box) rather than adding straight to cart.

## Things the brief says not to copy
Outcome percentages, ADHD trial comparisons, sampled-doctor endorsement, repeated near-identical reviews (their highlight carousel repeats the same 4 reviews), batch sold-out/reserved counters, "up to 71% off" stacked claims.
