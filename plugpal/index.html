Honest safety

It adds sockets, not power

Every outlet extender shares one wall circuit. Most sellers skip that part. Here's what belongs in PlugPal and what goes straight into the wall.

Plug into PlugPal

Everyday electronics and small appliances

  • Phones, tablets, watches, earbuds — on the USB ports
  • Lamps and routers
  • TV, streaming box, game console
  • Coffee maker — one heating appliance at a time
  • Robot vacuum dock, litter robot
  • Toothbrush and shaver chargers

Plug straight into the wall

High-heat and high-power appliances

  • Space heaters — always straight into the wall
  • Two heat appliances at once — kettle + toaster, air fryer + microwave
  • Hair dryers and straighteners
  • Window AC units
  • Laptops — USB-C is for phones and earbuds. Use your laptop's own charger.
  • Loose or damaged outlets — fix the outlet first

Everything on PlugPal shares one 15A / 1,875W wall circuit. If two heaters trip your breaker today, they'll trip it on PlugPal too. PlugPal isn't a GFCI, and its surge protection wears down over big surges. The indicator light shows it's still protecting.
