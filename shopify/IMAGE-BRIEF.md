# Mirevia product page: image brief

Use this brief to create or recreate images for the Mirevia™ The Melter product page (`templates/product.mirevia.json`). It's written so you can paste it into a new Claude chat or add it to a Claude Project. For the full design rules, add `DESIGN.md` too.

## The product
Mirevia™ The Melter: a cordless, wearable pad for period cramps that combines heat with vibration massage. It's a soft blush-pink pad on a grey/blush elastic strap, with a small black LED control display on the front. It's worn low on the belly or the lower back, over a light layer of clothing or against the skin.

## Brand look
- **Mood:** quiet luxury for sleep and recovery. Calm, confident, never shouty. Think Apple product pages and Therabody, not TikTok ads.
- **Light:** soft, natural daylight or warm lamp light at night. Gentle shadows, nothing harsh or flashy.
- **Backgrounds:** clean whites, soft cool greys (`#f5f5f7`), charcoal (`#272729`) for night scenes. The product supplies the pink, so the background shouldn't.
- **People:** real-looking women and people who menstruate, aged roughly 20–45, varied skin tones and body types. Relaxed and relieved, not posed or in pain.
- **Accent colour** (used sparingly, if at all): mulberry `#8a2f52`.

## Rules
1. **No text on images in the "Made for you" tabs or feature panels.** The page already has the headline beside or above each image, and text baked into a photo repeats it and gets cropped on phones.
2. Gallery images may carry one short line of text at most. Clean photos look more premium than ad-style graphics.
3. **One image, one idea**, matching its slot below.
4. No pink-bubblegum sets, no clinical blue, no hospital or medical props, no beige-and-brass styling.
5. No invented star ratings, review counts, "#1" claims or doctor endorsements. No medical claims: heat and massage "help tight muscles relax", they don't "treat" or "cure".
6. The product must look like the real one: blush pad, strap, black LED display.

## Prompt starter
Adjust the scene for each slot:

> Photorealistic lifestyle photo, square 1:1. A woman in her late 20s relaxing on a light grey linen sofa in soft afternoon daylight, wearing a blush-pink cordless heating pad on a soft strap low on her belly, over a cream knit top. The pad has a small black LED display. Calm, relieved expression. Clean, minimal, airy interior with white walls and cool grey tones. Quiet luxury, Apple-style product photography. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

## Putting images on the page
1. In Shopify, upload the images under **Content → Files**, or to the product's media for the gallery.
2. Open **Online Store → Themes → Customize** on the Mirevia theme. Pick the product page and the `mirevia` template.
3. Click the tab or panel and choose the image. Gallery order follows the product's media order.
